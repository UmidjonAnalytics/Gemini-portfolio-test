# Welcome to Your Portfolio!

This guide will help you manage your new data analyst portfolio. No coding experience is required!

## Logging In
1. Go to `yourwebsite.com/admin/login` in your browser.
2. Enter the email and password provided to you by your instructor.
3. You will arrive at the **Dashboard**, where you can see your profile completeness and recent messages.

## Changing Your Photo and CV
1. In the menu on the left, click **Profile**.
2. Under "Media", click **Upload Photo** to add a new profile picture.
3. Click **Upload PDF** to add your CV.
4. Don't forget to click the blue **Save Profile** button at the top right!

## Adding Your First Project
1. In the menu on the left, click **Projects**.
2. Click the **Add Project** button at the top right.
3. Fill out the details! You can add a title, cover image, and list the tools you used.
4. You will see three tabs for languages: **EN**, **RU**, and **UZ**. You can type English text in the EN tab, Russian in the RU tab, etc. If you leave RU or UZ empty, it will automatically show the English text.
5. Click **Save Project**.

*Note: Your new project will appear instantly on your live website. Go take a look!*

## Hiding or Reordering Items
- See the eye icon (👁️) next to a project, skill, or certificate? Click it to hide that item from the public site. It will be marked as a "Draft".
- See the grip dots (⋮⋮) on the left side of an item? Click and drag those dots up or down to reorder the list. The order saves automatically.

## When Sections are Empty
Don't have any certificates yet? Don't worry! If you delete all your certificates (or hide them as drafts), the entire "Certificates" section will vanish from your public website automatically. It will reappear as soon as you add one back.
